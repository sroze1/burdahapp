--- Full description ---
BurdahApp is a beautiful, distraction-free space for reading Islamic poetry, beginning with the Burdah of Sayyida Khadija RA — "بردة أم المؤمنين سيدتنا خديجة" — presented exactly as written, page by page, in its original Arabic calligraphy.

The app opens with a reverent animated splash: the golden domes of Madinah at sunset, the Bismillah rendered in Arabic, transliteration, and English translation, accompanied by a short recitation of Qari Abdul Basit 'Abd us-Samad reciting "Bismillahirrahmaanirraheem" — setting a calm, sacred tone before you begin reading.

Inside, you'll find:
- A faithful, page-by-page PDF reader that preserves the original Arabic calligraphy exactly as intended — no error-prone text extraction, just the authentic text
- Natural right-to-left page turns, swiping the way a physical Arabic book is read
- Double-tap to zoom in on any page for close reading of the calligraphy, with pinch to fine-tune
- An elegant Turkish/Ghazali-inspired geometric design throughout, with calligraphic Arabic typography
- Fully offline — the poem is bundled with the app, so there is no need for an internet connection to read

BurdahApp is built to grow: the underlying structure supports adding further Burdah poems and Islamic texts in future updates without disrupting the reading experience you already know.

Whether you are reading for reflection, study, or devotion, BurdahApp is designed to get out of your way and let the words speak — presented with the reverence the subject deserves, in sha Allah SWT.

BurdahApp collects no personal data, requires no account, and makes no network requests. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
