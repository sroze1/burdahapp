--- Description ---
BurdahApp is a beautiful, distraction-free space for reading Islamic poetry, beginning with the Burdah of Sayyida Khadija RA — presented exactly as written, page by page, in its original Arabic calligraphy.

Launch the app to a reverent animated splash screen: the golden domes of Madinah at sunset, the Bismillah shown in Arabic, transliteration, and English translation, accompanied by a brief recitation of Qari Abdul Basit 'Abd us-Samad — a calm, sacred moment before you begin reading.

FEATURES

• Faithful page-by-page PDF reading that preserves the original Arabic calligraphy exactly — no text extraction, just the authentic text as written
• Natural right-to-left swipe navigation, the way a physical Arabic book is read
• Double-tap to zoom into any page, with pinch to fine-tune, for close reading of the calligraphy
• Elegant Turkish/Ghazali-inspired geometric design and calligraphic Arabic typography throughout
• Fully offline — the poem is bundled with the app; no internet connection required to read

BurdahApp's structure is built to grow, so more Burdah poems and Islamic texts can be added in future updates.

Whether reading for reflection, study, or devotion, BurdahApp is built to let the sacred words speak for themselves, in sha Allah SWT.

BurdahApp collects no personal data, requires no account, and makes no network requests.
