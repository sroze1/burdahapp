# Privacy Policy for BurdahApp

**Last updated:** 2026-07-26

## Overview

BurdahApp ("the app") is a reading application for Islamic poetry. This
policy explains, plainly and completely, what the app does and does not
do with respect to your data.

## Data Collection

BurdahApp does **not** collect any personal data, in any form.

- **No account required.** The app has no sign-up, login, or user
  profile of any kind.
- **No personal information is requested, stored, or transmitted.** The
  app never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts,
  photos, or any other personal identifier.
- **No analytics or tracking.** BurdahApp does not use analytics SDKs,
  advertising identifiers, crash-reporting services, or any third-party
  tracking technology.

## Network Access

BurdahApp makes **no network requests** of any kind. All content — the
Burdah text (as a bundled PDF), images, and audio — ships inside the
app installation itself. The app functions fully offline and never
connects to the internet, a server, or any external service.

## Data Storage

BurdahApp stores no user-generated data on your device beyond what the
operating system itself manages for any installed app (e.g., standard
app cache used by the OS). The app does not write user data to any
remote server, cloud service, or third party, because it never
communicates over the network in the first place.

## Third-Party Services

BurdahApp does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or
APIs. It does not share data with — nor receive data from — any
external party, because no data leaves the device.

## Children's Privacy

Because BurdahApp collects no personal data whatsoever, it is safe for
users of all ages, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If BurdahApp's data practices ever change in a future update, this
policy will be updated accordingly and the updated version will
accompany that release.

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the developer
via the app's store listing contact information.
